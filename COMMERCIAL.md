# Commercial Licence

Humbaba is dual licensed. This page is for organisations that cannot use it
under the AGPL.

## Do you need one?

**You do not need a commercial licence if:**

- You are evaluating, experimenting, or building a prototype
- You use Humbaba internally and do not offer it, or a service built on it, to
  anyone outside your organisation
- You are building open-source software that you are willing to release under
  the AGPL
- You are an individual, a student, or a non-profit
- You are contributing to Humbaba itself

**You probably do need one if:**

- You run a modified Humbaba as part of a service your customers reach over a
  network, and you do not want to publish your own source code
- You embed Humbaba in a product you distribute under proprietary terms
- Your legal or procurement team prohibits AGPL dependencies, which is common
  in enterprises regardless of what the code is used for

The distinction that matters is AGPL section 13: if users interact with a
modified version over a network, they are entitled to its complete
corresponding source. For most commercial products that is not acceptable, and
that is what the commercial licence removes.

## What the commercial licence gives you

- The right to use, modify and distribute Humbaba without AGPL source
  disclosure obligations
- The right to keep your own modifications and surrounding code private
- Written terms your legal team can review

## What it does not give you

- Ownership of Humbaba
- Any warranty beyond what is agreed in writing
- Rights to the "Humbaba" trademark
- Exclusivity — the AGPL version remains available to everyone

## Honest notes

**On provenance.** An earlier version of this project contained a subsystem
whose origin could not be fully accounted for, and that was disclosed here
rather than buried. It has since been removed and replaced by code written
from a written specification. Every file in the repository can now be
attributed. The full record, including what the problem was, is in
`docs/PROVENANCE.md`.

**On maturity.** Humbaba is v0.3. It has 97 passing tests, three
interchangeable execution hosts, and measured benchmarks, and it has no
production users. `docs/LIMITATIONS.md` states what does not work. Read it
before committing to anything.
